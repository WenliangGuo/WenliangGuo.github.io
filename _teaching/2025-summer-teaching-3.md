## Course Description
This program is designed for K–12 students from across the United States and abroad, introducing the historical background, current developments, and research frontiers of artificial intelligence. It focuses on the specific field of computer vision, explaining what computer vision is, why it is needed, and its real-world applications. We have also designed several engaging and hands-on exercises incorporating the cutting-edge AI technologies, aimed at bridging theory and practice, helping students understand the mechanisms behind AI applications, and inspiring their curiosity about artificial intelligence.
